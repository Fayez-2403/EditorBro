From prompt to reel

  1. 01Prompt

    Describe the reel in plain words. A hook notices it's a video and loads the pipeline.

  2. 0264 skills

    Brief, hook, voice, motion, sound and QA playbooks, loaded only when a stage needs them.

  3. 031 pipeline

    Facts → brief → references → hook → voice → timeline → score → scenes.

  4. 0412 gates

    VERIFY to FINISH + SHIP. Every review round is scored, and the three worst problems are fixed with numbers.

  5. 05Render

    Measured, not guessed: loudness, cut sync, flat frames, speech-to-text.
